# Translation notes: clip_03_casino_story

- Japanese is translated from the unmasked English; asterisks appear only in the displayed English.
- Register: casual spoken male Japanese (〜だよ / 〜じゃねぇ), no invented dialect. 'You feel me?' is rendered as わかる？ or left implicit where it is only a filler.
- "axing" (AAVE for asking) is kept as spoken in the English. In Japanese it is rendered as 頼み込む (begging people for money), which is the meaning in context.
- "I wasn't even..." and "auntie, auntie...": the trailing words were too unclear to transcribe and are not guessed.
- Excluded as unintelligible or crosstalk: source 159.95-162.4, 164.6-169.25, 211.95-214.25 and 222.08-224.85.
- "It's the Emmys!" (Envy joking that the story deserves an award) is rendered as エミー賞もんだな！. An earlier draft misheard it as "image"; the independent critic caught it and it was verified with three models.
