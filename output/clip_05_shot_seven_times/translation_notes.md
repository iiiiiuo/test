# Translation notes: clip_05_shot_seven_times

- Japanese is translated from the unmasked English; asterisks appear only in the displayed English.
- Register: casual spoken male Japanese (〜だよ / 〜じゃねぇ), no invented dialect. 'You feel me?' is rendered as わかる？ or left implicit where it is only a filler.
- "I ain't f**king with you on seven" is rendered as 7発どころじゃねぇよ, conveying that it was really more than seven.
- "I be catching myself" and "done worn off" are kept as spoken in the English (AAVE is not normalized).
- "just... figured it out": the ellipsis marks a hesitation that is audible in the source.
